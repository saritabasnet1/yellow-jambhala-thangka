Educational Guides and Services

This journal offers educational guides for readers, collectors and practitioners who want to understand Yellow Jambhala thangkas. The guides are written in plain language, illustrated with original diagrams, and based on the paintings themselves and on the descriptions artists and teachers pass on.

Reading a Painting

A step-by-step guide to reading a Yellow Jambhala thangka: the mongoose, the skull cup, the yellow skin, the gold robes and the seated ease, explained in the order a teacher would point to them.

Understanding the Symbols

What each symbol means and how the symbols work together — the mongoose that showers jewels, the cup of nectar, the northern direction and the lineage to Kubera.

Choosing a Thangka

How to judge quality in drawing, colour and gold; how to choose a size and a variant, from a 45 by 60 centimetre piece to a 70 by 100 centimetre piece; and what to look for in a workshop.

Caring for a Thangka

How to hang a thangka, protect it from damp and strong light, dust it safely, store it if needed, and keep cotton canvas, mineral colour and gold in good condition for years.

How the Guides Work

The guides are the five long articles in the Blog, each one covering a subject in depth. Start with the first article for the meaning of the deity, then follow the symbols, the history and the practice. If you would like a guide on a subject we have not covered, or help reading a painting you own, please get in touch through the Contact page and tell us what you are looking at.
